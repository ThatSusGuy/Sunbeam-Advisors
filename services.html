We approach the lenders best placed to support a business (public and private sector banks, NBFCs and institutions) and negotiate terms on the client’s behalf.

03

Debt Restructuring

When a capital structure no longer fits the business, we help companies reset it, working constructively with existing lenders to protect value and continuity.

  • Corporate debt restructuringRescheduling, re-pricing and re-profiling of existing facilities
  • RefinancingTakeover of loans to reduce cost or extend tenor
  • Financial re-engineeringRebalancing debt and equity to improve liquidity and ratios
  • Lender negotiationProposals, viability studies and consortium coordination

The engagement

How a mandate runs

Every transaction is different, but most follow a clear path from first conversation to completion.

Understand

We study the business, its financials and its sector, and agree the objective and the right instrument.

Structure

We build the financial model, the structure and the information memorandum or proposal.

Approach

We take the opportunity to a focused set of lenders or investors and manage diligence and queries.

Close

We negotiate terms, coordinate documentation and stay involved through disbursement or completion.
