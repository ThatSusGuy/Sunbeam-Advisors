How we work

Senior attention, considered judgement

A boutique practice by design. Clients work directly with the people who structure and negotiate their transactions.

Partner-led

Every engagement is run by a qualified senior professional, not handed down. The person you brief is the person across the table from lenders and investors.

Research-led

We study the business and its sector before we approach the market, so that structures, valuations and information memoranda hold up to credit and investment scrutiny.

Institutional access

Long-standing relationships with private and public sector banks, NBFCs, private equity and mutual funds mean the right counterparties see the right opportunity.
