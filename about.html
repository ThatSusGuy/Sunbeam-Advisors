About the firm

A boutique advisory practice built on experience and discretion.

Sunbeam Advisors is a Mumbai-headquartered corporate finance advisory firm. We help companies raise and restructure capital and complete strategic transactions, drawing on two decades of investment banking practice and deep relationships with India’s lenders and investors.

Who we are

Investment banking judgement, without the overhead

Our team of Chartered Accountants, Company Secretaries and MBAs brings expertise in investment banking, IPO advisory, debt syndication and structured finance across manufacturing and services businesses.

We work with promoter-led and mid-market companies that need the rigour of a large institution with the attention of a small one. We take on a limited number of mandates at a time, research each business thoroughly before approaching the market, and stay involved through documentation and closing.

Our relationships span private and public sector banks, NBFCs, private equity funds, mutual funds and institutional investors, giving clients access to the right sources of capital for their stage and sector.

He has worked in investment banking and corporate advisory for about 25 years. Before founding the firm he held senior positions at some of India’s established investment banking and broking houses, working across equity and debt capital raising for mid-market companies.

His transaction experience includes SME IPOs and listings, debt syndication and structured finance, private equity placements, PIPE transactions, and mergers & acquisitions, across sectors from manufacturing and infrastructure to financial services and real estate.

As a Chartered Accountant, Company Secretary and Cost Accountant, he combines financial analysis, regulatory and corporate law knowledge, and cost discipline, which is useful in structuring transactions that lenders, investors and regulators will accept.

Our principles

What clients can expect

The way we work matters as much as the outcome. These principles guide every engagement.

Independence

Our advice is shaped by what is right for the client’s business, not by a product to sell or a balance sheet to fill.

Discretion

Financing and transactions are sensitive. Information is handled on a strict need-to-know basis, and client names are never disclosed without consent.

Rigour

Careful analysis, realistic projections and well-prepared documentation, so that proposals stand up to credit committees and investment committees.
